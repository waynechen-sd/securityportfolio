01 / Evidence correlation

01 ／訊號判讀

When signals disagree, establish a baseline and verifiable facts first.

當端點異常、平台流量與原站日誌各自呈現不同脈絡時，先建立客觀事實與基準線。

Platform records, origin-side records, related documents, and stakeholder observations often each describe only part of the same event. I first align their time ranges and characteristics, separate verified facts from open questions, and turn the result into a control direction and an observation plan.

面對 WAF/CDN 邊緣防護紀錄、伺服器原站日誌 (Origin Logs) 與應用層行為不一致的狀況，真正的挑戰在於各方往往基於片面訊號驟下結論。我透過對齊時間戳記與請求特徵，交叉比對異常行為，釐清盲區並建立精確的存取控制基準。

  • Cross-verifiable timeline and signal comparison
  • 可交叉驗證的事件時間線與訊號對照
  • Facts, open questions, and role boundaries made explicit
  • 明確區分已確認事實、待確認事項與角色邊界
  • A control direction that can be tested and monitored
  • 可測試、可持續觀察的控制方向
In manufacturing website scenarios, I used this method to investigate anomalous activity and mitigate repeated-download abuse driven by malicious automation without disrupting normal traffic.
在製造業異常排查中，我以此方法精準定位疑似 WebShell 路徑；並透過分析 TLS/JA4 指紋特徵重新設計防護邏輯，成功阻擋約 85% 的惡意自動化流量。

02 / POC delivery

02 ／POC 技術交付

When a POC stalls, step back and rethink the full project scope.

當技術驗證 (POC) 陷入停滯時，化解瓶頸並重塑可執行的交付路徑。

Technical evaluations stall when scope, network prerequisites, test conditions, owners, and acceptance criteria live in separate conversations. I structure them into an intake, dependency, tracking, and handoff system, with clear RACI ownership so the team can stay synchronized on the latest information.

技術驗證的停滯，往往肇因於範疇、網路環境、測試條件及驗收標準散落於各方溝通中。我的交付原則是：確保「零脈絡」的接手者也能一目了然——目前的缺口為何、責任歸屬，以及具體的驗收標準，讓專案順利推進。

  • Customer-facing documents for intake and launch confirmation
  • 客戶導向的文件，如需求與啟動確認材料
  • Test conditions, dependencies, ownership, and follow-ups
  • 測試條件、依賴關係、責任分工與 follow-up
  • Closure evidence and handoff-ready next actions
  • 結案依據與無縫交接的下一步行動
Over the past 12 months, I have led requirement convergence, technical coordination, tracking, and closure materials across 30+ enterprise security evaluations.
近十二個月，我主責 30+ 件企業資安技術驗證的需求收斂與結案材料；並針對大型專案制定涵蓋數個月的分階段導入計畫與關鍵查核點。

03 / Decision-ready data

03 ／決策資料判讀

When data cannot be compared, structure it to reveal common signals, then converge on a decision-ready basis.

當資料缺乏共通標準時，透過結構化萃取共通訊號，轉化為具體的決策依據。

Project notes, vendor responses, technical requirements, and test status are not decision-ready merely because they are collected in one place. I first define comparable dimensions—such as blocker, owner, success criteria, and next action—then connect the indicators back to their operating context.

若只是將缺乏共通標準的資料強行拼湊成表，往往只會得到「更整齊的混亂」。我會先萃取具備對齊價值的共通維度，並依據瓶頸點、責任歸屬、成功標準與下一步進行分層收斂，讓資料能實質支援決策。

  • Comparable field definitions and tracking structure
  • 可比較的欄位定義與追蹤結構
  • Make bottlenecks, reference indicators, and improvement recommendations transparent
  • 透明化癥結點、參考指標與優化建議
  • Priorities and plans the team can discuss together
  • 與團隊共同討論的優先順序與計畫
This approach supported quarterly POC reporting, technical-selection materials, and API Security prioritization in financial-services environments where regulatory requirements inform priorities.
這套方式應用於優化 POC 執行追蹤，並在應對金融業 Bot 盜刷與 DDoS 複合型威脅時，將 API 可視性與控制缺口轉化為對齊法遵的防禦優先順序。

Confidentiality機密性

Customer and order-related data should not be handled or shared casually.

客戶與訂單資料須嚴格控管，避免因作業便利而導致外流或散落。

Integrity完整性

Product details, pricing, visuals, and promotional messages need to remain correct and traceable.

商品資訊、價格與視覺內容必須維持正確且可追溯，確保對外溝通的一致性。

Availability可用性

A website is a customer-facing operational entry point; availability affects the entire flow, not just the technology.

網站是客戶接觸品牌與完成交易的入口；可用性的中斷影響的是整體商業命脈，而不單單只是 IT 技術問題。
